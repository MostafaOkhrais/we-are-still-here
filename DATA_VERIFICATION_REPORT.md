## 3. Detailed Discrepancies

### 3.1 Partnership Characterization (University of Sussex)
- **PDF 2/3 (p.13):** "ترتبط المبادرة بعلاقة تعاون أكاديمي ومجتمعي **غير رسمية** مع University of Sussex" — explicitly "non-formal/informal cooperative relationship"
- **Website (about.html):** "شراكة أكاديمية مع University of Sussex" — presents as formal "academic partnership" without the "informal" qualifier
- **Recommendation:** Trust PDF. Website should clarify "informal academic and community cooperation" to avoid overstatement.

### 3.2 Organizational Structure: 5 vs 6 Functional Areas
- **PDF 0 (p.3-4):** 5 functional areas explicitly listed. Area 5 (Student Support, Wellbeing & Scholarships) lead "to be confirmed" with interim oversight by Gaza Team Leader.
- **PDF 2/3 (p.4, p.5):** 6 departments listed including "اللوجستيات" (Logistics) as 6th.
- **Website (about.html, team.html):** Shows 6 areas matching PDF 2/3.
- **Analysis:** PDF 0 is a governance document for a specific period (2026-2027) with 5 areas because Student Support lead wasn't appointed yet. PDF 2/3 is the comprehensive institutional guide showing the full intended structure with 6 departments. They are consistent at different points in time / levels of abstraction.
- **Recommendation:** Trust PDF 2/3 for the comprehensive structure; PDF 0 for the specific governance snapshot. Website correctly reflects the full 6-area structure.
